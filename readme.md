## How to get verified

Almost every offer needs proof that you're a student. There are four common methods:

| Method            | How it works                                                                                                                      |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| School email (📧) | The fastest path. Some offers need a domain listed in an official school database, so a generic Gmail won't work.                 |
| GitHub Education  | Upload dated proof (student ID, class schedule or transcript) once to unlock the whole pack. Lasts two years, then you re-verify. |
| SheerID (🪪)      | A verification service used by Google, Figma and others. It may ask for a document if your school email isn't enough.             |
| ISIC card         | An international student ID accepted by some programs (JetBrains, for example) when you don't have a school email.                |

> [!TIP]
> Use a clear photo of a document that shows **your name, your school and a current date**. Most rejections are for missing dates or blurry photos.

## No school email

You still have options. **GitHub Education** accepts a student ID, class schedule or transcript with a current date, and it unlocks the biggest set of offers. **JetBrains** accepts an ISIC card or official documents. **SheerID-based offers** (Google, Figma and others) usually let you upload a document if your email isn't recognized.

It's also worth asking your school's IT office: many schools have student email accounts that students never activate. Meanwhile, everything in the "Free for everyone" section works with no verification at all.

## FAQ

**Do I need a credit card?**
Usually not. Azure for Students, AWS Educate and the GitHub pack don't ask for one. When an offer turns into a paid plan after the free period (like Google AI Pro), set a reminder to cancel.

**I'm in high school. What can I get?**
The GitHub pack (13+), GitHub Copilot Student, Figma, Autodesk and AWS Educate all accept high school students. Many others need university enrollment.

**What happens when I graduate?**
Most offers end when you can't re-verify. Export your data and move projects to free plans before your status expires.

**Can I use these for freelance or paid work?**
Many student licenses (JetBrains, Autodesk, Tableau Public) are for non-commercial learning only. Read each offer's terms before using it for client work.
